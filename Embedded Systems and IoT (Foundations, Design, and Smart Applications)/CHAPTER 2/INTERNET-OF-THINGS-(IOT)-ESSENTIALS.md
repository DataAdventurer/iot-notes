How does the Internet of Things differ from traditional internet-based computing systems?

The interconnectivity afforded by IoT connects physical devices and systems to the internet. This converts physical systems into intelligent ecosystems that can improve efficiency, support better decision making, and enable automation.

2.1
What does the scope of IoT include and why is it expanding rapidly across industries?

IoT services include remote surveillance, tracking, predictive maintenance, automation, and intelligent decision making. It is expanding rapidly because new innovations such as AI, cloud computing, and faster communication technologies make it possible to build smart ecosystems that are more efficient and connected.

2.2
How does IoT improve efficiency and automation in areas such as healthcare, agriculture, and smart cities?

By using sensors and well designed systems we can collect data from the environment. For example sensors in agriculture can measure soil moisture and nutrients. With this data systems can automate irrigation or enable predictive maintenance of machines.

2.3
Why is IoT architecture divided into layers and what purpose does this layered structure serve?

IoT architecture is divided into layers because each layer has specific function. When these layers work together they provide seamless flow of data from real-world environments to digital applications and services.

2.4
How do sensors, actuators, and smart devices interact to create an IoT system?

Sensors collect data from the physical world, smart devices process the data and actuators perform actions based on that data. Together they create system that allows data collection, decision making and real world actions.

2.5
How do different IoT communication models affect system performance, scalability, and latency?

Device-to-Device communication usually provides good performance and low latency but it is not very scalable. Device-to-Gateway can improve security and control but still has some limitations in scalability. Device-to-Cloud allows high scalability because many devices can connect to cloud services but it may introduce higher latency compared to local communication.

2.6
Why are specialized communication protocols required for IoT devices?

Different protocols serve different purposes. IoT devices often have limited power, memory, and bandwidth. Because of this we choose protocols based on the system needs. For example ZigBee or BLE are used for low power devices while Wi-Fi is used when higher bandwidth is required.

2.7
What role does an IoT gateway play in data acquisition, processing, and system security?

An IoT gateway collects data from devices and sensors and sends it to other systems such as cloud platforms. It can also perform protocol conversion, preprocess the data, and manage security functions in the system.
