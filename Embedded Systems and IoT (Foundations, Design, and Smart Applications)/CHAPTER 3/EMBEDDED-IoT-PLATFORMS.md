Why are specialized hardware platforms necessary for developing embedded IoT systems?

IoT platforms are the connection point between embedded systems engineering and networked computing. They enable devices that can sense, compute, communicate, and act in the physical world.

3.1
Why is Arduino widely used for prototyping and learning embedded IoT development?

Arduino boards are microcontroller based platforms designed for accessibility, rapid prototyping, and ease of use. They allow developers to quickly test ideas and build working prototypes without complex setup.

3.2
How does Raspberry Pi differ from microcontroller-based platforms in terms of capabilities and use cases?

Raspberry Pi is different from microcontroller platforms because it works like small computer. It can run a full operating system and operate like a gateway that connects devices to cloud services or other networks.

3.3
Why are low-power platforms such as ESP32 important for IoT devices?

Platforms such as ESP32 are important because they are highly integrated and power efficient. They include dual core CPUs, integrated Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and many peripherals which makes them suitable for connected IoT devices.

3.4
Why do IoT systems often use specialized operating systems such as FreeRTOS, Contiki, or TinyOS?

FreeRTOS is lightweight operating system designed for microcontroller systems. It provides task scheduling, mutexes, semaphores, queues, software timers, and APIs that allow multitasking even in small embedded systems.

3.5
What roles do firmware and middleware play in embedded IoT system architecture?

Firmware controls the hardware and implements the device logic. Good firmware is designed with modularity and clear layers. Middleware sits between firmware and cloud or applications. It abstracts device differences manages telemetry formats and handles data buffering and communication.

3.6
Why is cloud connectivity commonly integrated into IoT platforms?

Cloud connectivity is used so devices can send collected data to remote services. The transmitted payload may include device identifiers, timestamps, sensor readings, and other information used for monitoring, processing and authentication.

3.7
What lessons can be learned from practical IoT case studies such as smart irrigation or smart surveillance systems?

The importance of system reliability and security. For example systems must handle local buffering of data during network outages, manage storage correctly and ensure device security.
